# Pakistani Truck Horn Sound Library

## Project Overview

This sound library is part of my Generative Code university project, **Pakistani Truck Art Meets Berlin**.

The project explores how Pakistani truck art and traditional transport sounds can transform the visual and audio identity of Berlin's trams, buses, U-Bahn and S-Bahn.

## Cultural Context

Pakistani truck horns are not only used for road communication. Their distinctive tones, melodies and rhythms are also part of Pakistan's transport soundscape and cultural identity.

## Sound Collection

The collection contains 19 individual truck horn recordings.

Each sound will be studied based on:

- Pitch and frequency
- Rhythm and repetition
- Duration
- Emotional impact
- Possible cultural meaning
- Visual interpretation

## Generative Art and Sound Mapping

The sounds will be used in Resolume to control visual elements of Pakistani truck art.

| Audio property | Visual response |
|---|---|
| Volume | Brightness and intensity |
| Pitch | Color changes |
| Rhythm | Pattern animation |
| Repetition | Movement and visual loops |
| Duration | Animation length |
| Sound peaks | Visual transitions |

## Artistic Objective

The aim is to combine Pakistani cultural identity with Berlin's public transport system through generative art, sound-reactive visuals and video mapping.

## Research Approach

Individual horn meanings will be documented through personal observation and further cultural research. Artistic interpretations are not presented as verified traffic signals.
